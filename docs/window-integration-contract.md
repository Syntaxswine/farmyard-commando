# Placed window contract

Window integration is based on released main `6e47587`. The artist owns `dist/art/windows/catalog.js` and `geometry.js`; the coder owns the simulation, placement adapter and adaptation of the existing AFT shatter study. Ordinary walls stay 2 metres tall and .16 metres thick. The .12 m slab gives AFT a separate 2.12 m storey pitch; neither value caps a resizable glass sheet.

Coordinates are metres. Asset-local X runs across the span, Y is height above the supporting floor, and Z is the glass normal. Glass lies on local Z=0. A placement is `{id, definitionId, x, y, z:0, rotation:0|90|180|270, joins:{left:false,right:false}}`: x/y are Farmyard ground coordinates; rotation is Three's positive-Y rotation in degrees. Stable instance IDs are authored once and survive export/import. `r0c0` pane IDs repeat across instances, but break state never does. Plain glass across three wall units is one pane.

The runtime normalizes artist descriptors into `{id,width,height,bounds,panes:[{id,min:[x,y,z],max:[x,y,z]}],solids:[{id,role,min,max}]}`. Actual mesh handles and visibility remain in the artist's factory. Perimeter frames, mullions and waist walls are solid volumes; clear glass is transparent to LOS. A first accepted projectile impact breaks only the impacted pane and stops that round; later rounds can pass through the hole. Frames/sills remain meaningful impacts. Walking through windows remains unavailable, matching the existing wall-window rule; no vault or crossing action is introduced.

Authoring extension: `farmyardWindows:{version:1,instances:[...]}` on the existing AFT portable map. The Farmyard editor adapter uses the existing document, selection, undo/redo, renderer and export path. Pin snapshots remain untouched. The runtime map uses `windows`; operation state uses `windowBreaks:{instanceId:[paneId,...]}`. Saves retain those identities and break states and discard transient `windowEvents`. Existing maps without the extension retain their original behavior; legacy imported windows retain their previously released opaque-wall adaptation unless explicitly replaced by authored new windows.

This milestone supports flat receiving floors at world height 0. Upper floors, slopes and different receiving heights are rejected for authored windows. Shards are cosmetic and do not collide with nearby props or apply damage. The source effect's gravity, rigid triangles, wall clearing and bounce remain in metres, not nonuniformly scaled. Tiny sections receive fewer triangles; active meshes and total triangles are bounded on mobile.

The concrete artist dependency is `46a224b76ddeaf47156a7a118359530cc15cdd30` plus its completed catalog/flicker fix `0ef5b7186970a32397a0c61bdedca2ba24c17c79` from `codex/window-family` ([art PR #24](https://github.com/Syntaxswine/farmyard-commando/pull/24)). Both changes are included in this branch. Supported styles are `finish:steel|sage|ivory` and `wall:brick|concrete|corrugated`; `underlay` records replaced source edges for removal/undo. Joins use the artist's exact bounds; the authoring toolbar defaults to independent perimeter posts.

The clarified borderless contract is artist commit `20eeaa65c4f64a11c0c6ffb270363746812d3668` (local cherry-pick `4c86517`). Its dynamic ID is `window.full.borderless`; placements must retain `dimensions:{width,height}` in metres. `describeWindow(id,joins,dimensions)` and `library.build(id,{dimensions})` compute the actual bounds and a single `r0c0` pane with no solids, caps, posts, mullions or reflection strokes. The three `window.full.borderless.<1|2|3>m` presets default to 2.12 m high. Existing 36 framed IDs and their bounds remain compatible.

The editor explicitly converts horizontal 1 m tiles and vertical 2.12 m storeys. `20 × 5 metres` is `{width:20,height:5}`; `20 tiles × 5 storeys` is `{width:20,height:10.6}`. Runtime wall-boundary placement uses whole metre widths inside the 240 m sector; the artist factory itself accepts fractional dimensions. Height has no storey-count cap, but must be positive, finite and representable in render buffers with arithmetic headroom. Saved raw bounds are ignored and rebuilt from the validated definition/dimensions. Missing dimensions on the dynamic ID are rejected rather than silently shrinking a sheet to a preset.

Full declared bounds drive collision, the height limit for point shots, and residency; an outer portion intersecting a resident rectangle retains one asset even when its centre lies outside. There is no per-tile/chunk/storey pane subdivision or duplicate transparent root. The flat review map includes 20 m × 5 m and 20 m × 10.6 m sheets, a repeated independent sheet, and twenty legacy wall/window edges under one sheet that both adapters must suppress. Tall glass adds no walkable upper floor or vertical traversal.

Every covered legacy wall edge is suppressed, and adjacent 1.12 m wall overhang is clipped at the window endpoint in both collision and presentation. Editor clipping intersects existing parts; it does not stretch door jambs or alter upper-floor boxes. A 3 m pane remains one placed asset when it crosses a 24 m render seam.

Only accepted simulation impacts append an event. A burst invalidates geometry between rounds, so its first round can break glass while its later rounds hit a target through the hole. Grenades, RPGs and tank explosions visit independent panes using the existing flat-sector blast radius/occlusion policy; fuel blasts retain their existing wall-independent rule. Flame is blocked by intact glass, and no new heat-break rule is added. New window clipping and flame damage use the same horizontal jet at the operator's stance height, so a lower broken cell cannot damage a target through a still-clipped upper cone. The event seed is derived from instance/pane/sequence and consumes no combat randomness.

Live effects share a limit of six meshes and 210 triangles, with at most 70 triangles per pane regardless of its area. A simultaneous six-pane break shares that budget. Large/tall shards retain 7.8 m/s² gravity; their analytic lifetime extends through ground contact before fade instead of scaling gravity or stopping at the old 2.2 seconds. Canonical small-pane timing stays unchanged. Offscreen, reduced-motion and excess-budget shards can be omitted; damage remains authoritative. Audio is best effort after a user gesture, bounded to three simultaneous voices. Loading, mute or autoplay failure never delays combat. Effects are disposed on departure, restore and teardown; event receipts are not replayed when a chunk returns.

The authoritative animation study is `AnimalFactory3D/animation-studies/window-sneak`, source revision `ebf51393909040b61a7c40649d9d5f0ac3678735`. It contains a reusable animation and CC0 sound, not integrated gameplay damage/collision/save rules. This branch supplies those rules and records the borrowed source/audio hashes and license separately.
