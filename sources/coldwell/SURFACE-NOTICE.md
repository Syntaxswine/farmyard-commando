# Coldwell site map

The surface arrangement, roads, fence, support buildings, deterministic tree placement and terrain infill are original Nautilus artist work. This is a fictional surface compound, not a reconstruction of the historical museum grounds. The underground collection retains its original source commit `3b9d4004a7e51e5d345cded068fc46eab2f320a2`, notices and exact atlas; its complete original artist archive is included unchanged in the handoff. See `art/silo/NOTICE.md` inside that archive.

The surface uses the existing native grass/dirt/concrete materials, tree presets, edge barriers and metre kit from the released Nautilus v0.8.0 source `8c4670450cb2b38c1e1abd811d633c22de9e2b7c`. These references retain their existing notices and are not newly licensed or imported from a donor working tree. The map's diagrams and browser captures are original outputs.

Farmyard Commando revision `3a557bf743e5c5bb9728a3bee835ce85765c721a` was inspected through immutable Git objects. Its three-module importer closure remains only in ignored audit artifacts, not in the map distribution or runtime. The compatibility report records source hashes and demonstrates refusal of unsupported data. It does not grant a new license, change Farmyard, establish production adoption or claim the complete silo can be flattened into its current importer.
